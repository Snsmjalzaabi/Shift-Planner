Foxory Shift Planner helps shift workers organize their work schedule in a clear monthly calendar.

Plan day and night shifts, review your upcoming workdays, and keep your schedule easy to follow.

YOUR WORK CALENDAR
• See the whole month at a glance
• Add day, night and on-call shifts and days off
• Set start and end times, a location and a note for each shift

PLAN BEFORE YOU CONFIRM
• New shifts start as drafts, so you can plan without changing your confirmed calendar
• Confirm a shift when it is final
• Filter the planner by draft or confirmed shifts

IMPORT YOUR ROSTER
• Import a roster from a photo, a screenshot or an Excel file
• Choose your row and the app reads the dates and shift codes for you
• Review every date and code before anything is added; uncertain cells are never added without your check

SYNC ACROSS DEVICES
• Your shifts are saved to your Foxory account
• Sign in on another device to see the same schedule

FOXORY PLUS (optional monthly subscription)
• Plan shifts beyond the current month
• Export your shift plan as an Excel (XLSX) file
• Attach the export when you email your plan

The calendar, drafts, roster import and sync are free for the current month. You can delete your account and its data at any time from Settings > Legal.
